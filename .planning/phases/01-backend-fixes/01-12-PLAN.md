<objective>
BACK-15 (deploy wave 2, D-10): a confirm retry replays the original result instead of answering the client's own booking with SLOT_UNAVAILABLE.

RESEARCH F4: `confirmSchema` has no `booking_attempt_id`, so replay is keyed on columns that exist. Claude's discretion (CONTEXT: "attempt-keyed lookup of an existing booking vs a stored response"): an ownership-checked lookup of the existing booking by `payment_intent_id` (unique per booking; the PI itself is derived from the booking_attempt_id idempotency key), replying with the same 201 keys. No new column, no stored response.

Also on `createPaymentIntent` (the step iOS restarts at after a timeout, PAY-02): a conflict with the caller's own identical active booking returns a mappable `409 DUPLICATE_BOOKING` carrying the booking id, and the RESEARCH duration hardening is applied here (it was kept out of wave 1 because this is the payment path). Both shipped clients already map DUPLICATE_BOOKING ("A booking for this session already exists." on iOS build 6; make_a_booking_error_duplicate on Android), and build 6 decodes every active status in the replay body.

Canon note: ownership checks against replaying another client's booking are standard access control (IDOR), covered by the threat model and /gsd-secure-phase, not minted as a bespoke prohibition.
</objective>

<objective>
BOOK-06 (critical, planned first per the ROADMAP): a client can accept or decline an expert-proposed reschedule from the client Upcoming Booking details screen, and stays on that screen while it updates in place (D-01, D-02, D-03). Purpose: a `pending_reschedule` push tap lands on Upcoming Booking details, which today has no way to answer the proposal; the only Accept/Decline lives on Booking Summary.

Output: `RescheduleResponder`, `RescheduleResponseCard`, `UTF16Prefix`; the card on Upcoming details with a silent refresh; Booking Summary on the shared pieces with the UI-SPEC copy; shared test fixtures and two suites. The toast confirmation (`Reschedule accepted` / `Reschedule declined`) and the real status pill are plan 03-06.

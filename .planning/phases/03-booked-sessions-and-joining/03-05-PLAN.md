<objective>
Backend half of D-14 and D-16 for the expert side. D-14 (user decision 2026-11-10) says a booking the card was never charged for uses the "not charged yet" copy; the signal is the backend's derived boolean `payment_action_required` (from `paymentActionFields` in `src/utils/scaRecovery.js`; card authentication pending), plus `payment_status == "none"` on the client side. It applies to the client cancel preview and, by the same rule, to the expert cancel refund line (EXP-11), and the expert side fails closed: without the key, the expert sheet makes no client-refund claim. D-16 (user decision 2026-11-10, after the plan check) adds a second derived boolean, `payment_not_charged`, because `payment_action_required` reads false for a booking that holds no PaymentIntent at all: `convertToLongLead` moves an accepted-reschedule booking to `scheduled` with `payment_intent_id` null and voids the hold, and without this key the expert sheet would promise that client a full refund of money that was never taken. The client detail already carries `payment_action_required`; the expert details response carries neither key. CONTEXT allows this: "If research finds a backend gap, say so and make the change in the backend repo as a separate commit that stays safe for build 6 and current Android."

Purpose: without the keys, the expert app cannot tell a charged or held booking from one whose T-24h charge is waiting on card authentication (D-14) or one that holds no payment at all (D-16); plan 03-08 therefore shows the D-07 lines only where both keys read false.

Output: one additive backend commit carrying both keys (controller, tests, openapi) and a deploy handoff to the user. iOS decodes both keys in plan 03-08 as optional fields and fails closed while either is absent, so the iOS build is safe before and after the deploy.
</objective>

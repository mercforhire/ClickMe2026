Purpose: without the key, an expert cancelling a booking whose T-24h charge is waiting on card authentication is told "The client will get a full refund of {amount}", while nothing was charged.

Output: one additive backend commit (controller, tests, openapi) and a deploy handoff to the user. iOS decodes the key in plan 03-08, optionally, so the iOS build works before and after the deploy.

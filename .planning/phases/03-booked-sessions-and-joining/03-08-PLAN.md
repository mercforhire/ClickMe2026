<objective>
EXP-11: the expert cancel screen states the client's refund correctly. Per Q2 / Phase 1 D-17 (BACK-19, deployed), an expert-initiated cancel always refunds the client in full or releases the hold; the line now says which, with the amount (D-07), says nothing about money for a booking whose card authentication is pending (D-14) or that holds no PaymentIntent at all, such as one an accepted reschedule converted to long-lead (D-16), and says nothing about money when the payload cannot tell (D-14 fail closed: the backend keys from plan 03-05 are absent until it is deployed).

Purpose: the sheet says "A full refund will be processed to the client according to our policy" for every booking, including free ones, live holds where nothing is refunded, and converted long-lead bookings where nothing was ever taken.

Output: `ExpertCancelRefundLine` with tests, threaded through `ExpertCancellationClientBox`, `ExpertCancellationMainCard`, `ExpertCancellationView` and its view model (no default refund line anywhere), fed from `ExpertBookingSummaryView`; `ExpertBookingDetail.paymentActionRequired` and `.paymentNotCharged` decoded from the keys plan 03-05 adds. The states are exact: either key absent → no money line; `payment_not_charged` true → no money line; `payment_action_required` true → no money line; both present and false → the D-07 rules, including the hold-release line.
</objective>

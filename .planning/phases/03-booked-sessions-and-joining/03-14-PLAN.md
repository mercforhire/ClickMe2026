<objective>
BOOK-09 and the Upcoming half of BOOK-20's shared ID format. The Upcoming Booking details status pill stops hard-coding "Confirmed" and shows the booking's real status, mapped from the shared `BookingStatus` decoder case by case, with the raw string used only to humanize the decoder's `.unknown` fallback (Phase 2 BOOK-18). Purpose: the pill reads "Confirmed" for every booking, including pending and cancelled ones. The short ID is formatted inline twice in the view model; BOOK-20 needs the same format on Booking Summary and Android. This plan was split out of 03-06 to keep each plan at or under 10 files; `BookingShortID` moved here from 03-07 so 03-09 no longer waits on 03-07.

Output: `BookingStatusPill` with a full-case test; the pill wired on Upcoming details; `BookingShortID` with tests; Upcoming's ID on it.
</objective>

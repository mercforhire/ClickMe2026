EXPL-01 under D-10, and the `is_favorite` half of EXPL-05 under D-09. Today `ExpertProfileViewModel.loadProfileDetails()` swallows the details error with a best-effort read, so a deleted or suspended expert, or a network failure, leaves the seeded profile on screen as if it loaded; and the heart is set by scanning the first 100 favorites. This plan adds explicit not-available and load-failed states with Back and Retry, and reads the heart from the details response.

<objective>
PAY-02 under D-03, and the view-model half of PAY-04. Today any non-coded confirm failure falls into `handlePaidFlowError`'s default branch, which resets the paid flow and drops the PaymentIntent id, so a timeout after the hold was placed leaves the client with no booking on screen and a retry starts over. This plan classifies confirm failures, keeps the ids on an outcome-unknown failure, retries the same confirm twice, lands a replayed 201 on Request Sent, and shows the "We couldn't confirm your booking" alert when the retries run out.

Purpose: roadmap success criterion 1 ("A confirm that times out and is retried takes the user to their pending booking").

Output: `ConfirmOutcome`, `ConfirmRetryPolicy`, `PaidFlowAlert`, the status line and alert, two suites. The D-04 "maybe booked" copy and the D-15 DUPLICATE_BOOKING route are plan 04-13.
</objective>

<objective>
Gap 1 of 04-VERIFICATION.md (CR-01, PAY-02). The timed-out-confirm retry from plans 04-08 and 04-13 is safe only when the screen is left alone: `completePaidBooking` re-reads the live topic and slot on every user-driven re-run (Try again, the 3DS retry), `loadAvailability` clears the slot unconditionally, and nothing on the form is locked while the paid flow runs. A changed slot sends the original PaymentIntent with a different start time and moves or clears the D-04 ledger record; a month change makes Try again silently reset the flow and drop the PaymentIntent and card ids while a hold may exist.

This plan snapshots the attempt when the PaymentIntent is created (`PaidAttempt`), makes every confirm and the ledger key read only that snapshot, keeps the slot through a reload while submitting, locks the booking inputs while a booking is in flight, and adds the regression tests the verification asked for. D-03, D-04, D-15 and D-17 keep their meaning; only the source of the attempt's values changes.

Purpose: roadmap success criterion 1 ("A confirm that times out and is retried takes the user to their pending booking instead of showing 'slot taken'") holds even when the client touches the form during the confirm.

Output: `MakeABookingViewModel+PaidAttempt.swift`, the view-model and view changes, three relocated methods, ten new test rows, one device step and one UI-SPEC row.
</objective>

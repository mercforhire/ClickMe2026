<objective>
SIGN-06, second half of D-03 (one gate for every route into Expert mode). Plan 05-07 built the gate and wired Profile > Mode Switch on the client shell. This plan moves the remaining nine callers onto it: the other three Mode Switch entry points, both shells' push handlers, Splash's landing and Login's landing (whose duplicate setup checks are replaced by one shared `resolvePostAuthLanding`), then makes the mode setter private so no new view can bypass the gate.

Purpose: no path in the app reaches an unfinished expert dashboard, and a mode switch during a call is blocked everywhere.

Output: migrated call sites, `UserManager.resolvePostAuthLanding(roles:)`, launch rules in `ExpertModeGate`, a private setter, and new `ExpertModeGateTests` rows.
</objective>

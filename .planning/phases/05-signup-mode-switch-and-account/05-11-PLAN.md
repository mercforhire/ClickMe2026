<objective>
SIGN-02 (S3): the accumulator's `emailVerified` is only ever set by the Verify email screen, so resuming signup (launch resume, login resume, or the hub the gate opens) shows email verification as unfinished even when it is done. `ClickMeAPI.checkEmailVerified()` exists but has no callers. Seed the flag on the hub from `GET /auth/email/status` (the only source; `GET /expert/profile` has no such field), and from the signup response on a fresh signup.

Purpose: a user who already verified their email is never asked to do it again.

Output: `SignupAccumulator.refreshEmailVerified(using:)`, the hub's `.task`, the signup-response seed, and `SignupEmailVerifiedSeedTests`.
</objective>

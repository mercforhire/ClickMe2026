<objective>
D-02 promises that a failed setup lookup is retried "at the next launch or foreground". iOS has no foreground re-check today (the shells' scenePhase handlers only replay pushes). This is the small, cuttable plan RESEARCH Open Question 3 recommends: a `setupCheckPending` flag, Android's role-guarded `recheckSetupIfPending`, and an App-level `scenePhase` hook. Cutting it leaves the gate intact; only the foreground half of D-02 would be missing.

Purpose: an unfinished expert who launched offline is taken to setup once the network is back, without ever promoting a client-only account in the background.

Output: `ExpertModeGate.recheckDecision`, `UserManager.setupCheckPending` and `recheckSetupIfPending()`, the App hook, and the Splash pending mark.
</objective>

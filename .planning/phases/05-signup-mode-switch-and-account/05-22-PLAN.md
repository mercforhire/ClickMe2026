Make the whole Delete Account disclosure readable above the pinned buttons on the confirmation step (SET-06, UI-SPEC LT1). This plan is **separable**. Its item is not in 05-VERIFICATION.md's `gaps:` list. It is the human-verification item "SET-06 legibility", which VERIFICATION truth 12 records as UNCERTAIN because UAT test 2 was skipped. It is not an entry in VERIFICATION's `gaps:` list (that list holds only CR-01) and not a UAT `## Gaps` id (those are G-05-1 and G-05-6). The sources are VERIFICATION truth 12, 05-UAT.md test 2 (skipped) and 05-UI-REVIEW.md Top fix 2. This plan does not edit 05-VERIFICATION.md or 05-UAT.md. The evidence:

- 05-UI-REVIEW.md Pillar 5 says "WARNING — clearance contract failed", and Pillar 2 says "disclosure clipped by the pinned bar". Top fix 2 prescribes this exact change.
- The geometry is deterministic in code at HEAD. `DeleteAccountView.swift` L108-133 overlays `DeleteAccountBottomButtons` on the ScrollView in a bottom-aligned ZStack, and the scroll content ends with an 80 pt spacer (L115). The bar is about 163 pt tall and opaque (`DeleteAccountBottomButtons.swift`: divider 1 + top 12 + button 56 + gap 10 + Cancel 48 + bottom 36, on `DeleteAccountBrand.bg`). Whenever the step scrolls, the last 163 pt of the scroll view sits behind the bar, so about 83 pt of real content (the end of the "Kept" group) can never scroll into view.
- The step always scrolls at the largest Dynamic Type size, and UAT test 2 and checklist item 34 test exactly that size. LT1 ("the bottom spacer keeps the last line clear of the buttons") therefore fails by construction.

The fix: the bar becomes a bottom safe-area inset of the ScrollView, so the scroll view measures it and stops above it. At default sizes, where nothing scrolls, the screen looks the same. This is an iOS-only, single-view change, with a device re-test added to checklist item 34.

If the user prefers to wait for the device result first (UAT test 2 says "If the 'Kept' group is hidden, record it as a gap"), drop this plan. No other plan depends on it.

Purpose: a user deciding to delete their account can read everything that is kept, at any text size, before tapping Delete.
Output: one view change and one checklist re-test.
</objective>
